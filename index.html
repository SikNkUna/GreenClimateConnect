Radiations in the amount of energy the sun emits can affect Earth's temperature. While these cycles exist, they don't explain the rapid warming observerd in recent decades.

Large volcanic eruptions can release ash and aerosols(tiny particles) into the atmosphere which reflects sunlight back into space, causing a temporary coolimg effect.

Problems & Solutions

Global Warming

When coal , oil, and natural gas are burned for electricity, transport, and industry, they release large amounts of Carbon dioxide (CO2) and other greenhouse gases. To tackle this problem we need to shift to renewable energy sources like solar, hydro, wind, and geothermal(heat from earth's core).

Another thing we can do is that we improve energy efficiency in homes(turning off lights and unplugging unnecessary appliences), industries, and transport.

We also need to support clean technologies and carbon-capture systems.

Deforestation and Land-Use changes

This is the cutting down of forests for agriculture, urban-growth, and logging which reduces Earth's ability to absorb CO2, therefore increasing the rate of climate change.

The solution is to protect and restore(reforestate) forests in order to improve the convertion of CO2 to Oxygen

We need to support sustainable farming and forest management practices. People must reduce the buying of products linked to deforestation and find alternatives.

Industrial Agriculture & Waste 🏭

Livestock farming produces methane, and synthetic fertilizers release nitrous oxide, both of them potent greenhouse gases. Waste mismanagement also releases emissions.

Possible solutions include adopting climate-smart agriculture (precision fertilizing, cover crops).

People have to reduce food waste and improve composting.

One other solutions is that we shift towards more plant-based diets or sustainable livestock systems.
